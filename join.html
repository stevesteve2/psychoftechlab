Prospective PhD students

If you're a prospective PhD student, apply to the HCII PhD program. Feel free to email me in advance, but I will not have time to take meetings with prospective students in advance, and will also not have time to reply to all emails.

Postdocs

If you are interested in doing a postdoc in the lab, I encourage you to consider applying for external sources of funding (like this one), since it is unlikely we will have funded postdoc positions available (unless we are advertising them).

Undergrad & masters students

We also frequently work with undergrads and masters students as research assistants.
